# Phishing Email Awareness Guide

## What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which an attacker pretends to be a trusted person or organization to trick people into sharing sensitive information or opening harmful links or attachments.

## How to Identify Phishing Emails

- **Unknown sender:** Check the full sender email address carefully.
- **Urgent threats:** Be cautious of messages demanding immediate action.
- **Sensitive information requests:** Never share passwords or verification codes through email.
- **Suspicious links:** Check the destination carefully without opening unexpected links.
- **Unexpected attachments:** Do not open files you were not expecting.
- **Unusual language:** Look for spelling errors, strange wording, or mismatched branding.

## How to Stay Safe

1. Visit official websites by typing their address yourself.
2. Verify unusual requests using a known phone number or official contact.
3. Never reply with passwords or one-time verification codes.
4. Keep your browser, operating system, and security software updated.
5. Enable multi-factor authentication.
6. Report suspicious emails to your organization’s IT or security team.

## What to Do If You Clicked a Suspicious Link

- Stop entering information and close the suspicious page.
- If you entered a password, change it using the official website.
- If you shared banking or payment information, contact your bank immediately.
- Inform your IT or security team if the account belongs to your college or workplace.
- Run a security scan if you downloaded or opened an unexpected file.

## Conclusion

Pause, verify the sender, and never share confidential information through an unexpected email. When uncertain, confirm the request through an official channel.
